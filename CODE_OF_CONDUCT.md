# SEEMADRISHTI AI — Code of Conduct & Defense Ethics

## 1. Ethical AI Defense Commitment
SEEMADRISHTI AI is engineered strictly for perimeter security, territorial defense, anti-smuggling, and critical infrastructure protection. The systems, models, and protocols within this repository must adhere to ethical AI guidelines, ensuring accountability, transparency, and non-discriminatory processing.

## 2. Privacy & Data Protection
- CCTV video archives and edge camera streams must comply with statutory data retention and privacy mandates.
- Machine learning models must prioritize operational safety, transparent tracking, and verifiable chain-of-custody logging.

## 3. Community Standards
We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and professional environment for all contributors regardless of background, gender, or nationality. Constructive feedback, respect, and technical collaboration are foundational to this project.
